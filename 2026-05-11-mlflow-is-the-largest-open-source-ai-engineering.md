# MLflow: Open Source AI Engineering Platform

- Date: 2026-05-11
- Summary: MLflow is the largest open-source AI engineering platform designed for agents, LLMs, and ML models, providing tools to debug, evaluate, monitor, and optimize AI applications for teams of all sizes.

## Notes
MLflow is an open-source platform that provides a comprehensive solution for managing the end-to-end machine learning lifecycle. It is designed to help teams debug, evaluate, monitor, and optimize their AI applications, including those built with agents, Large Language Models (LLMs), and traditional machine learning models. MLflow aims to streamline the development and deployment of AI systems, making it a valuable tool for organizations working with machine learning at scale.
