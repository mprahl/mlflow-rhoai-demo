# What is an iPhone

- Date: 2026-05-08
- Summary: A summary of what an iPhone is, based on Wikipedia.

## Notes
The iPhone is a line of smartphones designed and marketed by Apple Inc. It was introduced by then-CEO Steve Jobs on January 9, 2007, and revolutionized the mobile phone industry with its multi-touch interface and lack of physical buttons (up to the iPhone 8 and 8 Plus, which had a single front button). Later models, starting with the iPhone 5s, integrated Touch ID.
